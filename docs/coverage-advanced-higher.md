# Advanced Higher Maths — reference coverage map

Systematic check that our course notes (`src/notes/data/advancedHigherMathsData.tsx`,
59 topics) cover everything taught in the reference material in `reference/AH/`
— four notebooks and three textbook units.

**Method.** Every section was listed from the reference files themselves, then
checked against our notes by reading the matching topic. Keyword counts were
treated as a hint only; several were false positives or negatives (e.g. "iff"
matching *d-iff-erence*, and "second order" missing our hyphenated
"Second-Order" titles).

> **⚠️ Read this before acting on the table.** The reference textbooks are
> teaching material, not the course specification — they contain revision of
> Higher, extension work and "further study" sections. When the equivalent N5
> table was reviewed, four of six reported "gaps" turned out not to be course
> requirements at all. So anything below marked **candidate** is *a question for
> you*, not a confirmed gap.

## Notes / questions for you

**Note 1 — Argand diagrams.** We refer to them (modulus as distance from the
origin, loci described on the diagram, roots of unity equally spaced) but never
teach "plot z = 3 + 2i". My assumption is this is assumed knowledge and the real
need is a *diagram*, which is already on the deferred diagrams list — but tell me
if you want it taught explicitly.

**Note 2 — Graph of inverse functions.** The reflection of y = f(x) in the line
y = x. We cover differentiating inverse trig functions, but not the graph
relationship. Is this required at AH, or Higher revision?

**Note 3 — Statement logic.** Textbook Unit 3 gives sections to implication
notation, negation of a statement, equivalent statements and two-way implication
(iff). We teach the proof *methods* thoroughly, but not this vocabulary. Given
the AH proof unit, I suspect negation and iff are genuinely required — but this
is exactly the sort of judgement I got wrong on N5, so I have not assumed.
